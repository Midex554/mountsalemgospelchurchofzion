About Us

We preach the undiluted Word of God, and every service is held under the guidance and power of the Holy Spirit. You are welcome to worship with us.

Our History

The Mount Salem Gospel Church of Zion Worldwide, Ori Oke Agbala Ewe, is a Bible-believing Church founded in the year 1984 under the name Cherubim and Seraphim (C&S;) Church of Zion. At inception, the Church was located at Aye Toro, Ebute-Metta, Lagos State, Nigeria.

The church is led by His Royal Spiritual Father in the Holy Temple, Dr. Prophet B.O Elebiju (JP) (DM), the King of Salem.

We uncompromisingly preach the undiluted Word of God and we conduct all our worship services under the absolute control, guidance and power of the Holy Spirit. The Church is governed by a distinct mode of dressing as divinely instructed, and operates under a comprehensive Constitution containing rules and regulations that bind every member of the Church and the Salem Community.

  1. Before 1990

    Affiliation with Hosannah Church of Zion

    In the course of its growth and pursuit of spiritual order, the church became affiliated with the Hosannah Church of Zion, founded by Oba Ademesingun Ikuesan, for spiritual fellowship and alignment within the Zion fold.

  2. 7 January 1990

    DIVINE RELOCATION TO THE PERMANENT SITE

    By divine revelation and direction of the Holy Spirit, on the 7th day of January, 1990, the Church relocated from Ebute-Metta to its present permanent and divinely ordained site known as Itedo Akogun Olodumare, Salem Community, Itun Oko Oba, Adekunle, Yaba, Lagos. Upon arrival at this new site, the Church transitioned from Hosannah Church of Zion and became AFFLIATED WITH THE UNITY CHURCH OF ZION UNDER THE ESTEMED LEADERSHIP OF BABA ALADURA F. S. ALAGBA. It was during this significant period that the Shepherd of the Church was ordained as Most Senior Apostle to the glory of God.

  3. 1993

    ESTABLISHMENT OF THE PRESENT NAME AND AUTONOMY

    In 1993, by the leading of the Holy Spirit and by divine mandate, the name of the Church was formally changed from C&S; Church of Zion to Mount Salem Gospel Church of Zion, Ori Oke Agbala Ewe, National Headquarters. Since then, the Church has operated as an autonomous National Headquarters and the global base of the ministry. The Church is under the humble and anointed leadership of His Royal Spiritual Father in the Holy Temple, Dr. Prophet Benson Omolere Elebiju, the King of Salem.

  4. Orderliness

    DOCTRINE, DRESS CODE AND GOVERNANCE

    We uncompromisingly preach the undiluted Word of God and we conduct all our worship services under the absolute control, guidance and power of the Holy Spirit. The Church is governed by a distinct mode of dressing as divinely instructed, and operates under a comprehensive Constitution containing rules and regulations that bind every member of the Church and the Salem Community.

  5. Celebration

    Anual Anniversary

    The Church, by divine ordinance, commences it Annual Revival and Anniversary Programme on the First Monday of January of every New Year. The aforesaid is succeeded by the Anniversary Celebrations of the Church's Glory of Zion Band, Cherubim Band, Choristers' Band and Aladura Band, in their respective order.
